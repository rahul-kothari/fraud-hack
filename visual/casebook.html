How to read a case

The bank checks every customer's name against watchlists. When a name comes close, it raises an alert about that customer. Each list entry that matched is a hit. You decide, hit by hit: is this the same person as our customer?

Each case shows the two sides, then the steps from the rules: find the customer and all their records, check their verified identity, read the hit, compare.

Coloured chips are hints: mechanical checks of single rules. They never add up to a decision. Practice-label cases have the answer key behind a button.
